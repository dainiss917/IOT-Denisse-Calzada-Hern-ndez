THIS IS US!!!

One Direction was a British-Irish boy band formed in 2010 on the TV show The X Factor.

The group consisted of Harry Styles, Niall Horan, Louis Tomlinson, Liam Payne, and Zayn Malik. They became one of the most successful boy bands of the 2010s.

IMPORTANT FACTS

-The band was formed in 2010.
-Zayn Malik left the group in 2015.
-They released five studio albums.
-The group began an extended hiatus in 2016 after which the members pursued solo careers.
-During their time together, they sold approximately 70 million records worldwide.
-Liam Payne died in 2024 at age 31.

HIGHLIGHTS

One Direction became a worldwide pop phenomenon during the early 2010s. Their debut album Up All Night reached number one on the Billboard 200. Some of their most famous songs include What Makes You Beautiful, Story of My Life, Night Changes and Drag Me Down. They completed several international tours and became one of the defining pop groups of their generation.
